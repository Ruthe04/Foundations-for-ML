# Foundations-for-ML
I use Jupyter Notebook to study. My process is: Study the content, either a book chapter or a lecture, and then I transcribe in my own words what I have studied. 
Personally, this method helps me to find where my understanding is still weak and what I already understand. 
I also do my practice exercises in Jupyter Notebook. 

This is the first notebook of a series, and I intend to write a separate notebook specifically about the philosophy behind this project.


AI Tools Disclosure!
As a self-taught student, I found the journey without a professor really challenging; that’s where AI tools came in, not to replace a professor completely, but to help me to the extent of their capabilities.  
I especially used ChatGPT and DeepSeek for the following tasks:

* Create customized lists of practice exercises and correct my answers.

The most important part of learning is not when we consume content, but when we are able to apply what we have learned; that’s why the Feynman technique was one of my methods. 
Furthermore, practice exercises are crucial to verify how much we actually learned and to identify where our knowledge is still weak.  
The approach I used was very simple: I sent the content in my Jupyter Notebook to the chat, and asked for a list of exercises based on the topic I studied.

* As an advisor.

I used these tools to discuss my interpretations and thoughts, to verify whether my understanding was correct, and to clarify subtopics that I didn’t understand within the main sources I was using.

I also used Grammarly, since English is not my native language. 
Since my main sources was always in English, copying and pasting everything would be untenable, so I strictly used it to improve my abilities.
